🏙️ Buddy City

Pick any place on Earth and Buddy City builds it in 3D from real OpenStreetMap data: every building with its real height and roof, the streets, pavements, crossings, rivers, parks, trees, lamps, benches and bus stops. Then it brings the city to life with traffic, people, boats, day and night, and a history slider.
